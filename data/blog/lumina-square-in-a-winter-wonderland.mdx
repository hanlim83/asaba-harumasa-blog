Lumina Square is absolutely buzzing today! You can feel the festive spirit in the air, even with all the crowds. The Christmas lights are twinkling, casting a warm glow on the freshly fallen snow. It's actually kind of… nice.

Yanagi seems to be in a surprisingly generous mood, suggesting we call off our meeting. Soukaku, of course, is already thinking about food (predictable, but endearing!). And Chief Miyabi, ever the efficient one, has found a way to combine business with pleasure – a Christmas feast right in the conference room! Leave it to her.

Even I have to admit, seeing the snow fall and the city lit up like this makes me want to just… relax. Maybe a day off wouldn't be so bad after all. Though knowing us, we'll probably still end up dealing with some Hollow shenanigans before the day is over.

Still, for a moment there, walking through the snowy streets with the team, it almost felt… peaceful. 

Merry Christmas (almost), everyone! 🎄✨
